Easy Gardening Guide

How to Use & Storage

🪴

New Plants

Fill the pot 80% with BaghVeda Potting Soil Mix, place your plant, and water lightly.

🌿

Old Pots

Remove the top 2–3 inches of old soil, refill with this organic mix, and water well.

💧

Watering

Water well the first time; after that, water only when the top soil feels slightly dry.

🛡️

Storage & Safety

Store in a cool, dry, shaded place away from direct sunlight. Keep away from children and pets.
